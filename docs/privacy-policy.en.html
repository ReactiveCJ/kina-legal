1. Who we are

Kina is a travel and AI try-on utility for visitors exploring China-related tools (visa info, essential apps, and related helpers) and optional AI Hanfu look generation.

2. Information we collect

Account (Google Sign-In). If you sign in with Google, we receive authentication data via Google and our auth provider (Supabase), which may include your Google account identifier, display name and email (as provided by Google), and session tokens. We do not receive your Google password.

Content you provide. Feedback submitted while signed in is stored with your user id on our servers. When you use AI Hanfu makeover, your selected photo is sent to our backend to produce a result. History and results may be kept on your device; you may also save images to your gallery.

On-device data. Language preference, visa-related country preference, sign-in session materials, credit/free-quota state, and local makeover history may be stored on your device.

Permissions. Camera and photo library access are requested only for makeover-related features you choose to use.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not intentionally collect precise GPS, contacts, or SMS for advertising.
